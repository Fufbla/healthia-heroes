🌱 HOW TO PLAY

🌱 Start with 5 seeds.
🌧️ Rain makes the soil wet.
🌳 Tap wet soil to plant. −1 seed.
🏜️ Tap dry soil and the seed is wasted.
🍎 Apples grow shortly after planting.
👆 Tap the tree's whole square to collect.
🌱 Every apple gives +2 seeds.
⏱️ Uncollected apples disappear.
❄️ Each full rain pass costs 2 seeds.
⚡ Rain gets faster each pass.
☠️ If you have 0 seeds when the rain finishes the final square, GAME OVER.
